# Stakeholder Register

This register identifies the people or groups who would be involved in a real ShopFlow checkout change. Roles are assumed for this fictional case study.

| Stakeholder | What they need | Involvement |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | Clear checkout, accurate total and order updates | Provides feedback on the proposed journey and participates in UAT |
| Store operations | Correct order and delivery information | Confirms order-handling needs and operational rules |
| Customer support | Clear status information to answer customer questions | Reviews customer-facing messages and common failure scenarios |
| Product owner | A useful first release with agreed priorities | Confirms scope, priorities and requirement changes |
| Development team | Clear and testable requirements | Reviews feasibility and implements the agreed workflow |
| QA / UAT tester | Testable acceptance criteria and expected results | Prepares and records test execution |
| Payment provider | Valid payment requests and clear responses | External dependency; confirms integration behaviour |

## Communication and decisions

- Confirm payment methods, delivery fee rules and delivery coverage with the product owner and store operations.
- Review failure messages and order-status wording with customer support.
- Review acceptance criteria with development and QA before build completion.
- Record UAT outcomes and defects before requesting business sign-off.

## Note

No real stakeholders were interviewed for this exercise. This register is a proposed stakeholder list based on the fictional scenario.
